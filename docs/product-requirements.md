# Kleanzo - Product Requirements Document (PRD)

> **TAGLINE**: DIRT GONE. SHINE ON.  
> **CORE POSITIONING**: YOU DESIGN THE SPACE. WE MAKE IT HANDOVER-READY.

---

## 1. Problem Statement
Architects and interior designers complete beautiful high-value spaces, but before client handover, site handovers are often stalled by tough post-construction cleaning challenges:
- Synthetic adhesives & Fevicol glue on veneers & marble
- Paint overspray on window glass & vitrified tiles
- Cement slurry & white grout haze on stone floors
- Wood dust & fine plaster in AC ducts and cabinetry

Generic household maid apps fail because they lack specialized chemical solvent knowledge, rotary machine equipment, and verified handover photo proof.

---

## 2. Product Vision & Target Audience
Kleanzo is professional cleaning infrastructure connecting design professionals with verified nearby cleaning agencies.

### Primary Customers:
1. Interior Designers & Studios
2. Architects & Architectural Firms
3. General Contractors & Project Managers
4. Luxury Homeowners & Commercial Clients

---

## 3. Core Functional Capabilities
- **Smart Search & Matching**: Multi-weighted recommendation score matching agencies by capability, distance, staff availability, and track record.
- **Stain Remediation System**: Diagnostic selector for Glue, Paint, Cement, Silicone, and Putty with safety notices.
- **Multi-Step Booking & Quote Wizard**: Instant booking for standard jobs or site quotation requests for complex handover projects.
- **Handover Proof & Rework Pipeline**: Mandatory before/after photo verification uploaded by agencies prior to client payout.
